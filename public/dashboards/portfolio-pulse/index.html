Metric definitions

Each metric is written once in the semantic layer. The tiles, charts, breakdowns and forecast all read the same definitions, so a number never changes depending on where you look.

earned_premium

Each contract's annual premium spread evenly across the days it is in force, summed by the month the cover was provided. A policy sold in December contributes one month of earned premium.

metric: earned_premium
type: simple
measure: sum(premium_earned_eur)
source: fct_premium_earned
grain: month × product × risk_zone
claims_paid

Settlement amounts in euros, dated by settlement date. Amounts arrive in mixed formats (1250.50€, €1250.50, 1250.50 EUR, $1375.55) and are standardised in staging before they reach this metric. Open reserves are excluded.

metric: claims_paid
type: simple
measure: sum(settlement_amount_eur)
source: fct_claims
filter: claim_status = 'settled'
loss_ratio

Claims paid divided by earned premium, calculated as a ratio of sums for whatever slice you select. Averaging monthly or per-segment ratios would give a different, wrong answer, so the layer never does.

metric: loss_ratio
type: ratio
numerator: claims_paid
denominator: earned_premium
target: 0.70
claim_count

Distinct claims settled in the period. Duplicate claim IDs from the source extract are removed in staging and counted by a test, so the dashboard shows how many were dropped.

metric: claim_count
type: simple
measure: count_distinct(claim_id)
source: fct_claims
filter: claim_status = 'settled'
